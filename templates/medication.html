💡 Medication Management Tips

  • Set Reminders: Use phone alarms or apps to remember medication times
  • Use a Pill Organizer: Organize medications by day and time for easy tracking
  • Keep a Record: Mark off doses taken to maintain adherence
  • Report Issues: Inform your doctor of any side effects or missed doses
  • Store Properly: Keep medications in cool, dry places away from children
  • Take with Food: Some medications should be taken with meals (as indicated)
  • Never Skip Doses: Consistent medication adherence ensures treatment efficacy
  • Refill in Advance: Plan ahead to avoid running out of medications
