💡 Physiotherapy Success Tips

  • Consistency is Key: Perform exercises regularly as prescribed for optimal results
  • Proper Form: Focus on correct technique over speed or number of repetitions
  • Gradual Progression: Increase intensity only when recommended by your therapist
  • Warm-up First: Always warm up muscles before starting physiotherapy exercises
  • Cool Down: Gentle stretching after exercises aids recovery and prevents stiffness
  • Pain Management: Ice or heat therapy may be recommended between sessions
  • Stay Hydrated: Drink water throughout your exercise sessions
  • Track Progress: Keep notes on your recovery to discuss with your physiotherapist
  • Be Patient: Significant improvements typically take 4-6 weeks of consistent effort
  • Communication: Report any concerns or new pain to your healthcare team immediately
