🚫 Foods to Avoid During Recovery:
- High-sodium processed foods (may cause inflammation)
- Excessive sugar and refined carbohydrates
- Alcohol (interferes with medication and healing)
- Caffeine in excess (affects sleep quality)
- Deep-fried foods (difficult to digest)
- Food allergens specific to your condition

💡 Practical Nutrition Tips for Recovery

  • Eat Small, Frequent Meals: 5-6 smaller meals instead of 3 large ones aids digestion
  • Meal Prep in Advance: Prepare healthy meals when energy permits for consistency
  • Include Protein at Each Meal: Supports tissue repair and maintains muscle mass
  • Eat a Rainbow: Different colored foods provide varied nutritional benefits
  • Limit Salt Intake: Reduces inflammation and supports cardiovascular health
  • Choose Whole Grains: Better sustained energy compared to refined carbohydrates
  • Stay Hydrated: Proper hydration accelerates healing and supports all body functions
  • Avoid Processed Foods: Prioritize whole, natural foods for better nutritional value
  • Balance Macronutrients: Include adequate proteins, carbs, and healthy fats
  • Listen to Your Body: Adjust portions and foods based on your recovery stage and appetite
